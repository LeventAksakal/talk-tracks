# Which languages the viewers pick, and whose voice narrates the demo video

## Question

The demo video script (`wiki/submission-video.md`, 2:58, multi-POV live session) has two choices left before the shoot:

1. Which languages do the friends pick on camera?
2. Who voices the narration between the live shots?

## Context

- Every translation is lazy now (decision 0035): a language costs nothing until a viewer picks it, then about
  $0.14 for a fully watched 17–20 min lecture. A take costs about $0.22, the whole shoot about $1.
- The rules allow an AI voice-over and require the video to be understandable in English.
- Beat 9 (a recording with a back-fill translation) is settled: the recording rooms are served on demand since
  the 11-03 deploy with the live-agents switch on.

## Options

**Languages**

- (a) The friends' own languages, e.g. Turkish and Japanese: real users reading their own language, the strongest
  impact story; judges may not read the scripts.
- (b) Spanish and Arabic: scripts judges recognise; less personal.

**Voice-over**

- (a) The owner's voice: matches the speaker on screen; needs a quiet recording session.
- (b) An AI voice: faster and allowed by the rules; sounds less personal next to live footage.

## Recommendation

Languages (a), voice-over (a).

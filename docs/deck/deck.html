1The problem

A live talk reaches only part of its room.

Language

Anyone who does not share the speaker's language gets nothing live.

Sight

A slide nobody describes is lost to a listener who cannot see it.

Time

Arrive late or look away, and a live stream has no way back.

AI CAN CLOSE EACH GAP, BUTcaptions, translation, slide descriptions and chapters, run for every talk in every language all the time, pay for output nobody reads.
SOeach AI job is a track in the live broadcast, and it runs only while someone subscribes to it.

2The transport

Why MoQT and not WebRTC, HLS or WebSockets

What the talk needs WebRTCan SFU, WHIP/WHEP LL-HLSsegments over a CDN WebSocketsa socket server MoQTone relay, what we use
Named tracks for media and data, side by side tracks and data channels; naming is per SFU one audio and one subtitle rendition at a time any bytes; the protocol is yours a broadcast is a set of named tracks
The publisher sees whether anyone listens depends on the SFU the origin never knows who stopped you build it the relay subscribes upstream only on demand
Fan-out through a cache SFU cascades, no caching any HTTP CDN every viewer is a socket on your server relays fan out and cache groups
Live and the past on the same track real-time only a DVR window; text tied to segments you build the history SUBSCRIBE the edge, FETCH a past group
Pushed as it is produced RTP and data channels the player polls playlists pushed each group on its own QUIC stream
Glass-to-glass latency, p50 * 336 ms 907 ms not measured 570 ms; text track 11 ms

What we accept: MoQT is still an IETF draft, so versions are pinned (moq-relay 0.17.8, agents on draft-18). Safari and iOS reach the relay over a WebSocket fallback. One CDN offers MoQ so far, in beta.

6Meta's models, per track

Two Meta models make every AI track

TrackModelWhat it doesRuns while
Captionsagents/captionsMuse Voice Transcriberealtime, endpointingTurns the speaker's audio into caption lines as they speak: the source text for every other AI track.any AI track below has a listener
Translationagents/translate-<lang>Muse Sparkmuse-spark-1.3, StandardTranslates each caption line into the language a listener picks, any of 50, with the three lines before it as context.someone has that language on
Slide descriptionsagents/describeMuse Sparkvision inputDescribes each new slide for a listener who cannot see it; a slide shown again reuses its description.someone has slide descriptions open
Chaptersagents/chaptersMuse SparkSplits the talk into chapters as it goes, explains its terms in a glossary, writes a recap for late joiners.someone has chapters open
RecordingsMuse SparkRe-punctuates a recording's caption lines without changing a word, so subtitles break on sentences.once per recording
